Hourglass in Houston fall: a set waist at 83 °F

Women’s clothes. Warm undertone against cool, same body. Petite is an inseam, not a URL.

An hourglass in this city, this month, is a waist that still reads at 82.8 °F and does not vanish when October’s normal drops 5.46 inches. A tunic erases it. Satin sticks to it. A petite pant that drops the rise to fix the length turns the X into a column.

October is the month this cut is for

82.8 °F by day, 60.9 °F at night, 5.46 inches at Bush Intercontinental. That is not coat weather and it is not a bare summer dress. The shirt stays tucked. The pant has a set waistband, not a fold-over pull-on: the fold sits on the waist and the hourglass goes slack. Cotton poplin or tropical wool. Not satin. Not flannel.

October workdayTucked shirt in olive or rust if the undertone is warm; burgundy or stone if it is cool. High-rise pant, set waistband. A belt only if it is no wider than the waistband. Closed shoe that can take a wet parking lot. No blazer through the middle of an 82.8 °F afternoon. A blazer then is costume. The rain shell stays in the bag until the storm, and it cannot turn the shirt sheer.

Petite is the inseam. The rise does not move.

US retail petite is cut for 5'4" and under. On this page that is a length, measured on the same 5'4" hourglass. It is not another address, and it is not published here as a national average.

28-inch inseam

The hem breaks once at the shoe. The rise stays at the natural waist. If the rise is dropped to shorten the pant, the waistband sits on the hip and the hourglass reads as a rectangle. That is the failure, not the inch.

32-inch regular

Same rise, same waistband, more length in the leg. Do not size up to get that length. Extra ease at the waist gaps at the back and the tuck collapses. The extra length is hem, not ease.

September is here so you do not wear this then

September, 90.4 °F and 70.6 °F, with 4.71 inches, is a tucked shirt and a shoe that can flood. Not the October pant in tropical wool. November, 72.6 °F and 51.5 °F, with 3.87 inches, keeps the same waist and adds a light jacket after dark. The jacket is open. A closed wool coat at noon boxes the waist you just built. Atlantic hurricane season is still open through November 30: the shirt cannot go sheer.

Wear and skip

  • Tucked shirt
  • Set waistband
  • Poplin or tropical wool
  • 28 and 32 inch on the same rise
  • Burgundy on a cool undertone
  • Satin
  • Tunic
  • Dropped-rise petite
  • Wool coat in September
  • Camel as the only color on cool skin
  • Anything that goes sheer when wet

Questions

What should an hourglass wear in Houston in October?

A tucked shirt, a set waistband, poplin or tropical wool. October at IAH is 82.8 °F, 60.9 °F at night, and 5.46 inches of rain. A tunic and satin both fail that month.

Does petite get its own page?

No. It is a 28-inch inseam next to a 32-inch inseam, same rise, on this page. Dropping the rise erases the hourglass.

Why two photos and not two URLs?

Warm and cool are the same body in October haze. Camel separates waist from hip on a warm undertone and goes dingy on a cool one. Both frames belong to /houston/fall/hourglass/.
