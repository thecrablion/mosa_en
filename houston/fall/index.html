Houston · Fall

Houston fall clothes start in October, not when the calendar flips

Women’s clothes. The normals are Bush Intercontinental. September is still a summer month.

At George Bush Intercontinental, September averages 90.4 °F by day and 70.6 °F at night, with 4.71 inches of rain. October is the first month this folder dresses for: 82.8 °F, 60.9 °F at night, 5.46 inches. November drops to 72.6 °F and 51.5 °F, with 3.87 inches. A coat that makes sense on a 51 °F morning is a bad idea on a September afternoon.

What every body shares

Satin sticks once the afternoon is still in the 80s and the rain is counted in inches, not in a mist. September does not get a wool coat. October does not get a summer dress that goes see-through when wet. November can take a light jacket after dark. The waist, the hem, and the hood change with the body. That is the next URL, not this one.

These pages do not publish a day count. IAH’s monthly total is the number we have. A month with 5.46 inches can be a few hard storms, not rain every afternoon. The outfit has to survive a downpour and still be wearable at 83 °F when the sun comes back.

Questions

Which months does this folder dress?

October and November. September is on the page so those clothes are not worn at 90.4 °F. September averages 90.4 °F and 70.6 °F, with 4.71 inches of rain.

Why a separate page for each body?

A belt that marks an hourglass pinches a rectangle, and a hood that is fine on a pear adds a second shoulder to an inverted triangle. The city and the season stay put.

Does Houston fall need a wool coat?

Not in September, and not at noon in October. November’s normal low is 51.5 °F. A light jacket after dark is the layer. A coat worn through an 82.8 °F afternoon is the wrong month.
