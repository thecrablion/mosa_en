My Own Style

What to wear, answered for the event - then seen on you

Eight occasions with real dress codes, answered from the room and the clock outward. Then the try-on: your photo, the garment, no guessing.

Style advice usually starts with a trend and ends with a garment that never leaves the closet. This site starts at the other end: the event you are actually going to, what the room requires, how many hours you will be standing, and who is in the photograph.

Eight events cover most of the searching women do about what to wear - funerals, weddings, interviews, concerts, showers, graduations, December parties and court. Each page ends with the same thing: a color and silhouette decision you can check before you buy, in the try-on, on your own body.

That last part is the only thing here a blog cannot do for you. Upload a photo and see the garment on it, at your height and your color, before the order goes through. Every page on this site is built to get you to that step with a decision already made.

Questions

What is this site for?

Answering what to wear for events that come with rules - a dress code, a venue, a security desk, a jury - and then letting you check the garment on your own photo before you buy it.

Is the try-on free?

The try-on is the app: upload a photo, see the garment on you. It starts free and needs no install.

Do you cover body type, petite or plus size?

Not as a topic of its own. The variables here are the room, the hours and the audience, which apply to everyone; fit advice by body is a different kind of site.

Why only eight events?

Because those eight are where the searching is, and each one has a rule to satisfy. When another event has real rules worth explaining, it gets its own page with a date.

Pick the event. Ten minutes later you know the color, the length and the shoe - and the try-on shows you the rest.
