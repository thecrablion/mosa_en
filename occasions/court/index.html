What to wear to court, and to a day of jury duty

You are being read by strangers for eight hours. Dress like the most credible version of your normal self, and bring the things the building does not have.

In a courtroom the outfit is not about respect for the building, it is about what a person in the jury box remembers. The answer, on both sides of almost every case, is the same: neutral color, closed and mid-length, nothing with a slogan or a logo, nothing that needs adjusting, and nothing that looks like an attempt. You want to read as a person who has a life, a job and a reason to be believed.

Jury duty adds a second problem: it is a full working day on hard chairs, with a cold room, a locked phone, no bag in some courthouses, and a lunch break that may be short. The woman who wins jury duty is not the best-dressed; she is the one with a cardigan, a refillable bottle, a pen, and shoes that survived eight hours of concrete.

Depositions, hearings, family court and a sentencing look similar from the outside and behave differently: a deposition is a full day in a conference room where comfort matters more and formality less; family court and anything with a judge deciding about your life lean more conservative, not less.

What this page covers. The safe baseline, what changes for a deposition versus a hearing, the bag and shoe problem, what courthouses do not allow in, and what to do if you are coming from work in scrubs or a uniform.

The baseline

Navy, gray, charcoal, camel, white or a muted solid. Trousers or a knee-length skirt or dress with a top under it, sleeves or a jacket, closed shoes with a low heel. Minimal jewelry, hair away from your face, makeup like your best normal day rather than a different face.

Eight hours, not thirty minutes

A layer you can keep on (rooms are cold and seats are hard), shoes with a sole, a bottle of water, snacks for the lunch break, a pen and a small notebook, and a physical book: phones get locked away and the waiting is long.

What the building decides

Many courthouses ban bags or require clear ones, and liquids are checked at security. Check the county page the night before, and if you are unsure bring nothing you cannot carry in a pocket. If you work in a uniform or scrubs, bring a change in a plastic bag and use the restroom before you go in.

Yes and no

  • Neutral, solid color
  • Shoulders covered, mid-length
  • Closed shoes with a sole
  • A layer for a cold room
  • Water, snacks, pen, book
  • Hair that stays put when you lean forward
  • Logos, slogans or political pins
  • Shorts, tank tops, flip-flops
  • Revealing necklines
  • New shoes on a full day
  • Heavy scent in a small room
  • A bag you cannot check at security

Questions

What should I wear to jury duty as a woman?

Comfortable and conservative: trousers or a midi skirt, a covered top or blouse, a cardigan or blazer, closed shoes with a sole, and a layer. Neutral colors, nothing you have to adjust while sitting.

Is black OK in court?

Yes. Black trousers with a solid top is a normal, credible courtroom outfit. What gets noticed is not black but a miniskirt, a slogan tee or shoes that will hurt you by two in the afternoon.

What about a dress?

A knit or woven midi with sleeves, or a shirt dress with a jacket, works better than anything with a slit or a low neckline. You will be sitting on a hard chair in front of strangers: sit down at home and look at it before you decide.

Do I really need a layer?

Courtrooms are cold and you cannot leave to get your coat, and a deposition room with four people and a videographer is hot. Bring something you can take off and put back on without undressing.

Can I bring a bag?

Often not. Many courthouses restrict bags or require clear ones, and everything goes through an X-ray. Assume you will carry a phone, keys, ID and a bottle in your hands, and check the county's list.

What if I am a party to the case, not a juror?

Then the stakes are different and the outfit should be quieter, not louder: solid color, mid-length, closed shoes, minimal jewelry, nothing that could be read as a statement about who you are.

Neutral color, closed and mid-length, one layer, shoes with a sole, and the things the building will not have: water, a pen, a book. Then sit down at home and check what the outfit looks like when you lean forward.
