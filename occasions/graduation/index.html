What to wear to a graduation, under the gown and after it

The graduate has one real problem: what to wear that will be photographed under a gown and still work when the gown comes off. Guests have it easier.

A commencement is 90 minutes outdoors on a hard chair, in May or June heat, followed by photographs, followed by a party that is often in someone's house. That schedule explains every choice below, and it explains why the classic mistake is dressing for the photograph and not for the two hours of standing in the sun.

If you are graduating: the gown covers almost everything except your shoes, your hair and your hands, and the only photograph that matters is the one where the gown opens. Wear shoes you can walk down steps in - an outdoor stage in July holds heat - a top you do not mind being seen, and hair that survives an hour under a cap. Skip the dress that shows when the gown swings open in wind.

If you are a guest: smart casual, dark or color, and the same rule as every outdoor ceremony - a heel that works on grass. Family photographs mean one tidy layer, not a full outfit change, and the party afterwards usually means you are holding a plate standing up.

Under the gown

Breathable fabric, a top you would show without embarrassment, and shoes - not socks - because the gown rises when you walk. Avoid a full skirt under it: the gown plus a skirt in a stadium seat is a heat problem, not a fashion one.

The cap and the hair

A cap sits on a flattened, sweaty head after 90 minutes. Wear something you can fix in a bathroom: a tie-back, a clip, a texture powder. Hairstyles that lose a fight with humidity are the mistake that shows up in every photograph.

Ceremony, then photographs, then party

Plan one outfit and change one thing: shoes, a jacket, an earring. A graduate who carries a second outfit ends up changing in a car. If there is a family dinner, a mid-length dress or trousers and a blouse is enough for both the ceremony and the table.

Questions

What do you wear under a graduation gown?

Something you would be photographed in: a solid top, trousers or a modest dress, and real shoes. The gown is the outfit; everything under it is the answer to a photograph, a hot stage and a stadium seat.

What should the family wear to a graduation?

Smart casual in color, low shoes, one layer. You will sit for an hour and stand for two more, and you will be in the background of hundreds of photographs, which is the moment to be quietly dressed rather than loudly dressed.

Is it OK to wear jeans to a graduation?

Dark jeans with a blouse and a jacket, yes, for a guest at a daytime ceremony. Not for the graduate, and not if the party afterwards is at a restaurant.

What do you wear to a graduation party?

One step down from a dinner party: a dress, or trousers and a nicer top. You will be holding a plate and standing, so shoes and a bag that stays put matter more than the silhouette.

Does it change for a community college or a master's ceremony?

Less dressing up, more practicality: smaller rooms, no stadium seats, and the same heat problem in July.

Dress for the ninety minutes, not for the photo: breathable under the gown, shoes you can walk in, hair you can fix. The photographs will take care of themselves.
